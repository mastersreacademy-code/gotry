# GoTry MVP scope
## Actors
- Parents discover activities and register interest for children.
- Providers submit an organisation, activity, trial and regular programme.
- Admin reviews listings and resolves exceptional situations.
## Five-step provider wizard
1. Business and contact details
2. Activity category, age range, description and venue
3. Trial format (group or individual), schedule/availability and interest deadline
4. Regular lessons, price, sessions and GoTry discount
5. Review, declarations and submission
## Proposed status lifecycle (not implemented)
Pending Approval -> Pending Registrations -> Proceeding - Pending Payments -> Proceeding - Paid.
Terminal outcomes: Rejected, Cancelled - Low Numbers, Cancelled by Provider.
## Decisions to confirm before payments
- Trial fee $2 appears as a reservation fee in mockups, but production fee has not been confirmed.
- Group trials require threshold logic, payment deadlines and waitlist reconciliation.
- Individual trials need a separate scheduling flow.
- Children data and provider verification need privacy, security and safety review.
## Safety
This is a non-transactional prototype. No live payment flow or production deployment configured.
